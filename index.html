ClearCut helps you remove a background from an image without installing software or creating an account. Use the free AI background remover to make transparent PNG images for ecommerce product photos, social media graphics, profile pictures, presentations, and personal projects. Upload a photo, let the AI detect the main subject, and download the result with transparency preserved.

Made for product photos, portraits, and everyday images

A clean cutout can make a product stand out in an online store, prepare a portrait for a profile picture, or help you place an object into a new design. For best results, use a clear photo with good lighting and visible separation between the subject and its background. Fine hair, transparent glass, motion blur, and low-contrast edges can be challenging for any automated background remover.

Private background removal in your browser

Image processing runs on your device using browser-based AI. Your selected image is not sent to our Worker API. The first use may take longer while the model files download; your browser can cache these files for subsequent sessions. Fast mode uses a smaller model to reduce download and memory requirements. High detail mode uses a larger model and may take longer or require more device memory.

Frequently asked questions

How can I remove a background from an image for free?

Choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP image, select a processing mode, and click Remove background. Download the transparent PNG when the result is ready.

Does ClearCut upload or store my photo?

No. The AI background removal runs in your browser. Your image is processed on your device and is not sent to the ClearCut Worker API.

Why does the first background removal take longer?

Your browser needs to download and initialize the AI model and runtime the first time. Those assets are cached by the browser, so later runs may be faster.

Can I make a transparent PNG?

Yes. The result is exported as a PNG, which supports transparent pixels. JPEG does not preserve transparency.

What image formats and sizes are supported?

PNG, JPG, and WebP images up to 15 MB are supported. Very large images may be resized before processing to help prevent browser memory errors.

Why did background removal fail?

Large images, low device memory, browser restrictions, and interrupted model downloads can cause failures. Try Fast mode, use a smaller image, close other tabs, and retry in an up-to-date browser.
